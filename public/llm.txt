## About Raahii Digital

Raahii Digital is a hospitality-focused digital marketing and growth agency based in Udaipur, Rajasthan, India, with a vision to work with hospitality brands across India.

The agency combines marketing, content, technology, branding and guest experience to help hospitality businesses become more discoverable, more memorable and less dependent on third-party booking platforms.

Raahii Digital is positioned as a Hospitality Growth Partner rather than only a traditional digital marketing agency.

## Core Philosophy

Hotels don't need more bookings. They need more reasons for guests to come back.

Raahii Digital believes that a booking is only the beginning of the guest relationship.

The Raahii Way:

1. GET THE GUEST
2. SERVE THE GUEST
3. BUILD THE BRAND

The goal is to help a hotel move beyond simply acquiring bookings and build a stronger relationship with its guests.

## Core Idea

When a traveller thinks of the destination, your hotel should be the one they remember.

Raahii Digital focuses on building this connection before, during and after the guest's stay.

## Raahii Companion

Raahii Companion is a Raahii Digital product designed to improve the guest experience at partner hotels.

It provides guests with local guidance, destination recommendations and itinerary support so that travellers can better experience the destination during their stay.

The concept connects the hotel guest with local knowledge and assistance through a convenient digital experience.

Raahii Companion can help guests with:

- Local destination recommendations
- Itinerary planning
- Places to visit
- Things to do
- Food and local experiences
- Trip guidance
- On-call local assistance
- Guest support through WhatsApp

Raahii Companion represents the "SERVE THE GUEST" stage of the Raahii Digital philosophy.
